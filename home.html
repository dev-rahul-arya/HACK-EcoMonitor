Features

Everything You Need for Environmental Monitoring

Comprehensive tools to track, analyze, and respond to environmental conditions in real-time

Air Quality Monitoring

Track AQI, PM2.5, PM10, O₃, NO₂, SO₂, and CO levels with real-time updates and historical trends.

  • Real-time AQI calculations
  • Pollutant breakdown analysis
  • Health impact assessments

Water Quality Analysis

Monitor pH levels, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, TDS, and conductivity for safe water assurance.

  • Multi-parameter testing
  • Contamination alerts
  • Safe drinking indicators

Weather Tracking

Get temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed, UV index, and weather forecasts for any location.

  • 24-hour forecasts
  • Severe weather alerts
  • Global location search

AI-Powered Insights

Leverage Gemini AI for intelligent analysis, anomaly detection, and actionable recommendations.

  • Trend predictions
  • Anomaly detection
  • Health recommendations

Automated Alerts

Receive instant notifications via email when environmental conditions exceed safe thresholds.

  • Customizable thresholds
  • Email notifications
  • Emergency protocols

Data Visualization

Beautiful charts and graphs to visualize trends, compare data, and make informed decisions.

  • Interactive charts
  • Export reports
  • Historical analysis
